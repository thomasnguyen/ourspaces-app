Layout · statement

One idea per slide.
Said plainly.

The headline is a full sentence broken over two lines. The lede says it again with more words, for anyone who looked away.

  • Every bullet opens with a bold phrase, so the slide reads from the bold alone.
  • Three or four bullets. A fifth means it's two slides.
  • One lime highlight per slide, on the line you want remembered.
